# FAQ

Customers can update billing contacts from account settings or by contacting support. Support may request account verification before changing billing details.

To cancel a subscription, customers should submit a cancellation request before the renewal date. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically issue a refund.

Invoices can be resent to the billing contact on file. If the customer cannot access the billing mailbox, support should verify account ownership before changing the contact.

Feature requests should be logged with customer context, business impact, and examples. Urgent operational blockers should be escalated separately from ordinary feature requests.
