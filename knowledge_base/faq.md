# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

# Purpose

This document provides standardized responses and operational guidance for the most common customer inquiries received by SenAI CRM. It serves as a knowledge source for AI-powered assistance, Customer Success, Support, Sales, and Operations teams to ensure accurate, consistent, and policy-compliant communication.

---

# General Principles

- Always provide clear and concise responses.
- Verify customer identity before discussing account-specific information.
- AI may answer informational questions but must not make contractual or legal commitments.
- Billing, compliance, legal, and security matters should follow their respective policy documents.
- Enterprise customers may have custom contractual terms that supersede general policies.

---

# Account Management

## How do I update my billing contact?

Customers can update billing contacts through the account settings portal or by contacting support.

Before making changes:

- Verify account ownership
- Confirm authorized administrator status
- Record the request in the audit log

Enterprise accounts may require additional approval.

---

## How do I change my account administrator?

Support should verify:

- current administrator
- requester identity
- organizational authorization

Unauthorized requests must be rejected.

---

## How do I reset my password?

Customers should use the password reset link.

Support should never:

- request passwords
- send passwords via email
- manually disclose credentials

If account compromise is suspected, escalate to Security.

---

# Subscription Management

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancellation requests should be submitted before the renewal date.

Cancellation:

- stops future renewals
- does not automatically generate refunds
- does not delete customer data immediately

Refund eligibility is governed by the Refund Policy.

---

## Can I reactivate my subscription?

Yes.

Customers may reactivate before permanent account closure.

Pricing may differ if promotional pricing has expired.

---

## Can I upgrade my plan anytime?

Yes.

Upgrades are prorated and become effective immediately.

---

## Can I downgrade immediately?

Generally no.

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal cycle unless an approved exception exists.

---

# Billing

## Can invoices be resent?

Yes.

Invoices may be resent to the verified billing contact.

Identity verification is required before changing billing recipients.

---

## I cannot access my billing email.

Support should:

- verify ownership
- update billing contact if authorized
- resend invoice

Never disclose invoices to unauthorized users.

---

## I was charged twice.

Possible duplicate billing should be:

- verified
- escalated to Finance
- reviewed under the Refund Policy

AI may acknowledge the issue but cannot approve refunds.

---

## Can payment dates be extended?

Only Finance or Customer Success may approve payment extensions.

AI should never promise exceptions.

---

# Refunds

## Am I eligible for a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on:

- billing history
- contract terms
- trial status
- duplicate charges
- verified billing errors

See Refund Policy for details.

---

## How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds generally process within 7–10 business days.

Actual settlement depends on the payment provider.

---

## Can AI approve refunds?

No.

AI may recommend actions and draft replies but cannot authorize refunds.

---

# Pricing

## Are discounts available?

Available programs may include:

- annual billing discounts
- nonprofit pricing
- academic pricing
- startup programs

Enterprise discounts require Sales approval.

---

## Can AI negotiate pricing?

No.

Pricing negotiations require Sales involvement.

---

## Do Enterprise customers receive custom pricing?

Yes.

Enterprise pricing depends on:

- user count
- contract length
- integrations
- support level
- compliance requirements

---

# Product Features

## Can I request a new feature?

Yes.

Feature requests should include:

- business use case
- customer impact
- workflow description
- urgency

Support should log feature requests for Product review.

---

## Why is a feature unavailable?

Possible reasons:

- plan limitations
- feature flags
- phased rollout
- regional restrictions
- pending release

Support should verify entitlement before responding.

---

# Integrations

## Why does my integration fail?

Common causes:

- invalid API key
- missing scope
- incorrect webhook configuration
- signature validation failure
- rate limiting

Refer to API Integration Guide.

---

## Can support debug custom code?

Support provides guidance but cannot debug customer applications.

Complex integration issues should be escalated to Developer Support.

---

# Analytics

## Why are dashboard numbers delayed?

Analytics processing may be asynchronous.

Small delays are expected during:

- imports
- large sync jobs
- maintenance

Persistent delays should be escalated.

---

# AI Features

## Why did AI classify my email incorrectly?

Classification depends on:

- email content
- thread history
- retrieved knowledge
- confidence score

Low-confidence cases may require human review.

---

## Why wasn't my email auto-replied?

Possible reasons:

- low confidence
- legal concern
- GDPR request
- security incident
- enterprise escalation
- policy restriction

The AI prioritizes safety over automation.

---

## Can I edit AI-generated replies?

Yes.

Human edits are encouraged for sensitive communications.

Edits may be logged to improve future AI recommendations.

---

# Security

## I accidentally shared my API key.

Immediately:

- rotate the key
- notify Security
- verify recent activity

Do not continue using compromised credentials.

---

## I suspect unauthorized access.

Immediately:

- change credentials
- notify Security
- preserve evidence
- review audit logs

Critical incidents receive priority handling.

---

# Compliance

## Can you export my data?

Data portability requests require:

- identity verification
- legal review
- compliance workflow

AI may acknowledge receipt but cannot complete the request.

---

## Can you delete all my data?

Deletion requests require:

- verification
- legal review
- retention validation

Do not process automatically.

---

# SLA

## My production system is down.

Production outages should be classified according to the SLA Policy.

Critical incidents receive immediate escalation.

Support will provide status updates until resolution.

---

## Can I receive service credits?

Service credits depend on contractual obligations and management approval.

AI cannot promise compensation.

---

# Spam & Suspicious Emails

## Why didn't I receive a reply?

Spam, phishing, and malicious emails are intentionally excluded from automated responses.

These messages are quarantined and logged for security review.

---

# Enterprise Customers

Enterprise customers receive:

- Account Manager
- priority routing
- advanced support
- enterprise SLA
- compliance assistance
- procurement support

Custom contractual obligations may apply.

---

# AI Agent Guidance

Before answering customer questions, retrieve:

- FAQ
- relevant policy document
- CRM profile
- thread history

AI should provide factual, policy-grounded responses and recommend escalation when required.
