# Compliance FAQ

# Purpose

This document serves as the internal compliance knowledge base for SenAI CRM. It assists AI agents and support teams in responding consistently to enterprise security, privacy, legal, and regulatory inquiries while ensuring that legally sensitive matters are escalated appropriately.

---

# Guiding Principles

* Compliance commitments must always reflect documented company policy.
* AI-generated responses are informational only and must never provide legal advice.
* Regulatory obligations take precedence over automation.
* Sensitive compliance requests require human review.
* All compliance-related interactions should be recorded in audit logs.

---

# Supported Compliance Frameworks

SenAI supports or provides documentation for:

* GDPR
* HIPAA (where Business Associate Agreements apply)
* SOC 2 Type II
* Data Processing Agreements (DPA)
* Vendor Risk Assessments
* ISO 27001 (where applicable)
* Enterprise Security Questionnaires

Support staff should avoid making commitments beyond approved documentation.

---

# GDPR Frequently Asked Questions

## What is a GDPR data portability request?

Under GDPR Article 20, customers may request a copy of their personal data.

Required process:

1. Verify requester identity.
2. Record the request.
3. Escalate to Privacy/Legal.
4. Track fulfillment timeline.
5. Respond through approved channels.

The AI may acknowledge receipt but must never confirm legal completion.

---

## What is the Right to Erasure?

Customers may request deletion of personal data under GDPR Article 17.

Deletion requests require:

* identity verification
* legal review
* retention policy validation
* audit logging

Never delete data directly based on an email alone.

---

## Can AI approve GDPR requests?

No.

The AI should:

* acknowledge receipt
* recommend escalation
* cite this policy
* avoid legal commitments

Final decisions belong to Privacy or Legal.

# HIPAA

## Does SenAI support HIPAA workloads?

HIPAA-related customers require evaluation of:

* Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
* Protected Health Information (PHI)
* encryption controls
* audit logging
* access controls
* retention policies

Do not claim HIPAA certification beyond approved documentation.

---

## What should support ask?

* Is PHI stored?
* Is a BAA required?
* What integrations are used?
* Required retention period?
* Regulatory deadline?

Escalate enterprise healthcare opportunities to Compliance.

---

# SOC 2

Customers may request:

* SOC 2 report
* trust principles
* audit period
* controls overview

Reports should only be shared through approved channels.

Do not email restricted reports without authorization.

---

# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Enterprise customers may request a DPA before onboarding.

Required information:

* legal entity
* jurisdiction
* processing purpose
* subprocessors
* retention requirements

Legal review is mandatory.

---

# Vendor Security Questionnaire

Enterprise procurement often requires:

* encryption details
* access control
* backup strategy
* incident response
* disaster recovery
* penetration testing
* vulnerability management

Support should gather context and route to Compliance.

---

# Data Residency

Customers may ask:

* Where is data stored?
* Can data remain in-region?
* Are backups regional?
* Cross-border transfer policies?

Do not speculate.

Reference approved infrastructure documentation.

---

# Encryption

Standard guidance:

* Encryption in transit using TLS
* Encryption at rest using industry standards
* Secrets stored securely
* Access controlled by least privilege

Do not disclose implementation details beyond approved documentation.

---

# Audit Logging

Critical events recorded include:

* escalations
* AI recommendations
* approval actions
* login events
* administrative changes
* security incidents

Audit logs support investigations and compliance obligations.

---

# Incident Response

Security incidents include:

* credential exposure
* ransomware
* phishing
* unauthorized access
* API key compromise
* webhook replay
* suspected data breach

Immediate escalation to Security is required.

The AI should generate only an acknowledgement.

---

# Privacy Requests

Examples:

* account deletion
* export my data
* remove my information
* privacy complaint
* retention dispute

All require identity verification.

Never process automatically.

---

# Compliance Edge Cases

Mandatory human review:

* conflicting retention instructions
* requests from unauthorized users
* court orders
* subpoenas
* law enforcement requests
* procurement contract modifications
* custom legal language
* government sector procurement

# Frequently Asked Questions

### Can AI approve compliance requests?

No.

### Can AI provide legal advice?

No.

### Can AI acknowledge compliance emails?

Yes, using approved language only.

### Are GDPR requests automatically fulfilled?

No. Identity verification and legal review are required.

### Should compliance emails be auto-replied?

Only with an acknowledgement when policy allows. Final commitments require human approval.
