# Compliance FAQ

SenAI supports enterprise security reviews for HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2, DPA, and vendor risk assessments. Compliance questions from enterprise prospects should include deal context, seat count, deadline, and requested framework before routing to the compliance owner.

GDPR Article 20 data portability requests are legal obligations. Acknowledge receipt, verify requester identity, avoid debating the request in email, and escalate to privacy/legal workflow. Do not auto-reply with final legal commitments.

HIPAA-related inquiries require confirmation of business associate agreement needs, protected health information handling expectations, audit logging, encryption controls, and data retention requirements. Do not claim HIPAA certification beyond documented policies.

Compliance edge cases include conflicting retention instructions, requests from unauthorized users, public-sector procurement forms, and customers asking for custom contract language. These require human review.
