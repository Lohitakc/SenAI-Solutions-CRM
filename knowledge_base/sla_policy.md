# SLA Policy

Production incidents are classified by business impact. Critical incidents prevent customers from using core CRM workflows and require immediate escalation. High priority incidents degrade important workflows but have a workaround.

Initial response targets are 1 business hour for critical issues, 4 business hours for high priority issues, and 1 business day for normal support requests.

Resolution times depend on severity, root cause, and third-party dependencies. Support should provide regular updates until closure. Customers should receive clear status, mitigation steps, and next actions.

Repeated SLA breaches should be escalated to the support manager and account owner.
