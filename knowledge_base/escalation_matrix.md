# Escalation Matrix

Critical cases require immediate human ownership. This includes ransomware, legal threats, GDPR requests, suspected data breach, production outages, SLA breach claims, public review threats from active customers, and VIP churn risk.

Security escalations go to the security incident owner. Legal and GDPR matters go to privacy counsel. SLA breach and P0 production incidents go to support leadership and the assigned account manager. Pricing negotiations above standard discount bands go to sales leadership.

Never auto-reply to critical legal, ransomware, GDPR, or security cases. The AI may draft an internal summary, recommended next steps, and a customer-safe acknowledgement, but final response must be approved by a human.

Escalation examples:
- P0 outage with financial loss: critical support escalation and account manager notification.
- Chatbot misinformation about refunds: support leadership review and policy-cited correction.
- VIP renewal on hold: account manager and customer success escalation.
- Spam or cold outreach: no customer response required unless manually approved.
