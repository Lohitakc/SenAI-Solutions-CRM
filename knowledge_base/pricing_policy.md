# Pricing Policy

SenAI Solutions CRM offers monthly and annual billing for sales, support, and operations teams. Standard plans include contact management, email workflow support, analytics, and knowledge-base search.

Annual contracts receive a predictable fixed rate for the contract term. Plan upgrades are prorated from the date of change. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle unless a written exception is approved by customer success.

Invoices are issued at the beginning of each billing period. Payment is due within 15 calendar days unless the customer contract states otherwise. Late payments may pause non-critical services after written notice.

Customers can request invoice corrections by contacting support with the invoice number, billing contact, and reason for correction.
