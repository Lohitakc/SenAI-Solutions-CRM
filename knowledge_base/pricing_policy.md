# Pricing Policy

# Purpose

This document defines SenAI CRM's pricing structure, billing rules, discounts, contract policies, upgrades, downgrades, invoice handling, and pricing negotiation guidelines. It serves as the authoritative reference for Customer Success, Sales, Finance, Support, and AI-powered customer interactions.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Pricing should remain transparent and consistent.
- AI may explain pricing but must not negotiate beyond approved policy.
- Enterprise pricing requires human approval.
- Discounts outside published programs require Sales authorization.
- Billing commitments should always align with signed contracts.

---

# Available Plans

## Starter Plan

Designed for small teams beginning customer relationship management.

Features include:

- Contact management
- Email tracking
- Basic analytics
- Basic AI assistance
- Standard support

Recommended for teams with fewer than 10 users.

---

## Growth Plan

Designed for growing businesses.

Includes:

- Everything in Starter
- Advanced analytics
- Workflow automation
- AI-powered classification
- Knowledge base search
- Team collaboration
- API access
- Priority support

Suitable for organizations with 10–100 users.

---

## Enterprise Plan

Designed for large organizations requiring scalability and compliance.

Includes:

- Unlimited automation workflows
- Dedicated Account Manager
- Enterprise SLA
- Advanced security controls
- Compliance support
- Custom integrations
- SSO
- Audit logging
- High-volume API limits
- Executive support

Enterprise pricing is customized.

---

# Billing Cycles

Customers may choose:

- Monthly billing
- Annual billing

Annual contracts generally receive preferential pricing compared to monthly subscriptions.

Annual pricing remains fixed during the contract period.

---

# Payment Terms

Invoices are generated at the beginning of each billing cycle.

Standard payment terms:

- Net 15 days

Enterprise contracts may specify alternative payment terms.

Late payments may result in suspension of non-critical services after written notice.

---

# Plan Upgrades

Customers may upgrade at any time.

Upgrade pricing is prorated.

The new plan becomes active immediately after confirmation.

Unused value from the previous subscription is credited toward the upgraded plan where applicable.

---

# Plan Downgrades

Downgrades become effective at the next billing cycle.

Current subscription benefits remain available until renewal.

Immediate downgrades require Customer Success approval.

---

# Seat Expansion

Customers may purchase additional seats during an active subscription.

Additional seats are prorated.

Enterprise customers may receive negotiated volume pricing.

---

# Seat Reduction

Seat reductions become effective at renewal.

Unused seats during the billing cycle are generally not refundable.

Exceptions require Customer Success approval.

---

# Invoice Corrections

Invoice correction requests should include:

- invoice number
- account email
- billing contact
- explanation
- supporting evidence

Finance reviews all correction requests.

---

# Duplicate Billing

If duplicate billing occurs:

1. Verify payment records.
2. Verify invoice history.
3. Escalate to Finance.
4. Process correction or refund.
5. Notify customer.
6. Record audit log.

Duplicate billing receives high operational priority.

---

# Accepted Payment Methods

Supported payment methods include:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- ACH Transfer
- Bank Transfer
- Approved Enterprise Purchase Order

Alternative payment methods require Finance approval.

---

# Discounts

Approved discount programs include:

## Annual Discount

Annual subscriptions may receive reduced pricing compared to monthly billing.

---

## Non-Profit Discount

Eligible registered non-profit organizations may receive discounted pricing after verification.

Supporting documentation may be requested.

---

## Academic Discount

Qualified educational institutions may receive special pricing.

Eligibility verification is required.

---

## Startup Program

Approved startups may qualify for promotional pricing during defined eligibility periods.

Promotional pricing expires according to program terms.

---

## Volume Discounts

Large deployments may qualify for volume discounts.

Approval requires Sales Leadership.

AI should never promise volume discounts.

---

# Enterprise Negotiation

Enterprise pricing may consider:

- user count
- contract length
- implementation complexity
- security requirements
- compliance requirements
- support level
- custom integrations

Only Sales leadership may approve negotiated pricing.

---

# Renewal Policy

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before renewal.

Customers receive renewal reminders before billing.

Enterprise renewals may involve contract review.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Cancellation prevents future renewals.

Cancellation does not automatically generate refunds.

Refund eligibility is governed by the Refund Policy.

---

# Pricing Negotiations

AI may explain:

- available plans
- included features
- billing cycles
- invoice process

AI must not:

- negotiate discounts
- approve custom pricing
- promise future discounts
- modify contracts

Pricing negotiations above published discounts require Sales escalation.

---

# Currency

Invoices are generated in the contract currency.

Exchange rate fluctuations do not modify agreed contract pricing.

---

# Taxes

Applicable taxes depend on customer jurisdiction.

Tax exemptions require supporting documentation.

Finance determines tax treatment.

---

# Enterprise Procurement

Large organizations may require:

- vendor registration
- procurement approval
- security review
- compliance review
- legal review

Sales and Legal coordinate procurement activities.

---

# AI Agent Guidance

When responding to pricing questions, the AI should retrieve:

- Pricing Policy
- CRM Profile
- Account Status
- Thread History

The AI may:

- explain plans
- explain billing
- explain upgrades
- explain renewal policy

The AI must not negotiate pricing.

Yes.

---

---

## Can AI negotiate pricing?

No.

Pricing negotiations require Sales approval.

---

## Can invoice corrections be requested?

Yes.

Finance reviews all correction requests.

---

## Do Enterprise customers receive custom pricing?

Yes.

Enterprise pricing is negotiated individually.
