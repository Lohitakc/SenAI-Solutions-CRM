# Escalation

Escalate immediately when an email mentions legal action, compliance, data breach, security incident, payment dispute, executive complaint, or repeated SLA failure.

Critical priority issues should include a concise summary, customer account, impact, affected workflow, timeline, and recommended next action.

Customer success owns billing disputes and refund escalations. Engineering owns reproducible product defects. Security owns suspected data exposure or credential compromise.

When escalating, draft a customer-safe acknowledgement and an internal reasoning summary. Do not promise outcomes that require approval.
