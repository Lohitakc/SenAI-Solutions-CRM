# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This document defines SenAI CRM's refund eligibility criteria, approval workflow, escalation process, customer communication guidelines, and exception handling procedures. The objective is to ensure refunds are processed consistently, fairly, and in compliance with contractual, financial, and legal obligations while maintaining customer trust.

---

# Guiding Principles

* Every refund request must be evaluated objectively using documented policy.
* Customer satisfaction should be balanced with contractual obligations and financial controls.
* AI-generated recommendations are advisory and must never bypass approval requirements.
* High-value, legal, or enterprise refund requests require human review.
* Refund decisions must be fully auditable.

---

# Standard Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be approved under the following circumstances:

* Duplicate charges for the same billing period
* Incorrect invoice generation caused by system error
* Payment processed multiple times
* Plan cancellation during an officially approved trial period
* Billing errors confirmed by Finance
* Service unavailability resulting in contractual SLA violations
* Contractually agreed refund clauses
* Regulatory or legal requirements

Supporting evidence should include:

* Account email
* Invoice number
* Transaction identifier
* Payment date
* Description of issue
* Supporting screenshots or documentation when available

---

# Standard Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds are generally not issued for:

* Partial usage after the billing cycle has started
* Customer misunderstanding of product features
* Missed cancellation deadlines
* Unused subscriptions
* Feature requests not yet implemented
* Training or onboarding time
* Third-party integration issues outside SenAI control

Exceptions require Customer Success and Finance approval.

---

# Enterprise Customer Exceptions

Enterprise customers may qualify for discretionary refunds or service credits when:

* Contractual obligations are not met
* Strategic account retention is necessary
* Executive escalation occurs
* Multiple verified SLA breaches occur
* Significant production incidents impact business continuity

Enterprise refund decisions require:

* Account Manager approval
* Customer Success approval
* Finance approval

High-value refunds may additionally require executive approval.

---

# Trial Period Policy

Customers within an approved trial period may request cancellation and refund if:

* The request is submitted before trial expiration
* No contractual exclusions apply
* Abuse or repeated trial creation is not detected

Repeated refund requests across multiple trial accounts should be flagged for fraud review.

---

# Duplicate Charge Workflow

If duplicate billing is suspected:

1. Verify invoice IDs.
2. Verify payment gateway transactions.
3. Confirm duplicate processing.
4. Notify Finance.
5. Initiate refund.
6. Notify customer.
7. Record audit log.

Duplicate charge refunds should receive high operational priority.

---

# Goodwill Credits

In situations where a refund is not contractually available but customer satisfaction is important, Customer Success may offer:

* Service credit
* Extended subscription period
* Additional seats
* Temporary plan upgrade
* Professional services credit

Goodwill credits require manager approval above predefined limits.

---

# Public Reputation Risk

If a customer threatens:

* negative public reviews
* social media campaigns
* industry forum complaints
* press exposure

the issue should be escalated to Customer Success leadership.

The AI may recommend escalation but must not negotiate compensation autonomously.

---

# Legal Threats

Emails containing statements such as:

* "I will sue"
* "My lawyer will contact you"
* "Legal notice"
* "Court action"
* "Consumer protection complaint"

must be escalated immediately.

The AI may generate an acknowledgement only.

Final responses require human approval.

No automatic refund decision should be made.

---

# Chargebacks

When a customer initiates a chargeback:

* Suspend refund processing
* Notify Finance
* Gather transaction evidence
* Preserve audit logs
* Coordinate with payment provider

AI should recommend manual review only.

---

# Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be considered when:

* only part of a service failed
* implementation was incomplete
* contract allows prorated adjustments

Finance approval is required.

---

# Refund SLA

Target processing times:

| Stage                   | Target                  |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Initial acknowledgement | Within 4 business hours |
| Eligibility review      | 1 business day          |
| Finance approval        | 2 business days         |
| Refund initiation       | 1 business day          |
| Payment settlement      | 7–10 business days      |

Enterprise contracts may define different SLAs.

---

# Escalation Rules

Automatically escalate when:

* legal action mentioned
* regulatory complaint
* enterprise customer
* VIP customer
* refund exceeds approval threshold
* repeated refund requests
* suspected fraud
* duplicate billing affecting multiple invoices

Never auto-approve these requests.

---

# AI Agent Guidance

The AI agent should:

* retrieve this policy before drafting refund replies
* verify thread history
* consider CRM profile
* evaluate churn risk
* evaluate account value
* recommend escalation when required
* provide policy-grounded reasoning
* generate customer-safe acknowledgement drafts

The AI must never promise a refund before human approval when approval is required.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### Can customers receive refunds after 30 days?

Normally no, unless contractually required or approved as an exception.

### Can Enterprise customers receive exceptions?

Yes, subject to Account Manager and Finance approval.

### Can AI approve refunds?

No.

AI may recommend actions and draft responses but cannot authorize refunds.

### Can duplicate charges be refunded?

Yes, after verification.
