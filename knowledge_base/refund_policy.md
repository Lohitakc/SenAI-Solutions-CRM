# Refund Policy

Refund requests are reviewed by customer success and finance. Eligible refund cases include duplicate charges, billing errors, plan cancellation within an approved trial period, or documented service unavailability that violates the service agreement.

Refund requests should include the account email, invoice number, charge date, and reason for the request. Approved refunds are usually processed to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.

Refunds are not typically issued for partial usage after a paid billing period starts unless required by contract or law. Enterprise refunds require approval from the account owner and finance lead.

Urgent refund complaints should be prioritized and routed to customer success.
